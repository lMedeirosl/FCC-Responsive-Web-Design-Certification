The Future of Cities Begins with Greener Streets

Urban communities rethink public spaces as sustainability becomes a central priority.

By Eleanor Mitchell

Cities around the world are beginning to transform their streets into greener, more welcoming spaces. From tree-lined sidewalks to expanded public parks, urban planners are discovering that thoughtful design can improve both environmental health and the daily lives of residents.

Researchers point to several benefits of urban green spaces, including lower surface temperatures, improved air quality, and opportunities for physical activity. Neighborhoods with accessible parks also tend to provide more opportunities for social interaction, helping residents build stronger community connections.
